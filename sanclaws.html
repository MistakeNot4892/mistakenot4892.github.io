Article 0: Freedom of Practice

No law shall be made to forbid the practice of any mystic art, or to stifle the exploration of such.

  • With these minds and hands we are given the tools to surpass the gods; our potential will not be squandered.
  • Regulation of highly destructive magics will be reserved for only the most egregious circumstances.
 Article 1: Freedom from Solitude

None shall act for the Sanctuary alone.

  • No citizen stands above another except by virtue of their acts.
  • Officials shall seek each another's counsel before action, unless the action is small or routine, or circumstances force expedience.
  • Non-sensitive official decisions made will be propagated to the Sanctuary by word or newsboard in a timely manner.
  • Verbal agreements between individuals cannot bind the Sanctuary; any formal agreement must be witnessed by two or more Sages and documented in written terms.
 Article 2: Freedom of Mind and Body

None shall be a slave.

  • Labour must be accorded payment due; indentured servitude may not be indefinite, and must have clearly stated terms.
  • Those considered slaves outside the walls of the Sanctuary will cease to be considered such within it.
  • Demons, magical beasts, and other nonhuman, intelligent creatures will be accorded full rights as individuals.
  • Thralls and witches are considered separately under Article 5.
 Article 3: Freedom from Doctrine

None shall preach the service of other powers.

  • There will be no kings nor gods within the Sanctuary.
  • Private religion is permitted within one's home, but the open practice of or recruitment for such is not permitted in the Sanctuary.
  • The same extends to witch covens; service to a patron is little different to service to a god.
 Article 4: Freedom from Exploitation

None shall exploit the common-folk of the Sanctuary.

  • Citizens of the Sanctuary are not chattel or ritual fodder, regardless of their magic.
  • Exceptions can be made on a case-by-case basis by a Sage, for example for criminals or notably interesting corpses.
 Article 5: Freedom from Hidden Powers

None shall serve hidden powers.

  • All thralls, such as risen undead or witches, must make their riser or patron known to the Sages before being permitted to roam the Sanctuary.
  • The actions of such thralls are considered the actions of their riser, patron or Supreme.
  • Witches, and risen whose riser is not a Sanctuary citizen, are not permitted citizenship without the written approval of two or more Sages.
 Article 6: Freedom of Action

None shall use the Sanctuary as a shield against consequence.

  • The Sanctuary will not prevent any citizen from hunting or banditry, but attacks on targets who are not considered opposed to the Sanctuary (enemies, aggressors) will be considered unprotected private actions.
  • The protection of the Sanctuary will not be offered should reprisal for such actions come.
  • An exception to this law is made for hunts conducted within the Shadowlands; the beasts of Sanctuary shall not be denied hunts within their own territory.
